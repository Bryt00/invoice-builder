1. Pay-As-You-Go Credits

Credits are purchased in advance via Paystack to fund invoice dispatch and PDF generation on Teks-Invoice. Credits do not carry expiration dates and remain in your account until used.

2. Refund Eligibility

Unused credit bundles purchased within the last 14 days may be eligible for a full or partial refund upon request, provided no invoice dispatches have been made using the bundle.

3. Transaction Adjustments

In the event of duplicate billing or system errors during Paystack checkout, our support team will credit your account balance or initiate a transaction reversal.
